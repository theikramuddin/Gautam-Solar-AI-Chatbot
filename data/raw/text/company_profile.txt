GAUTAM SOLAR — COMPANY PROFILE
================================

ABOUT US
Gautam Solar is one of India's leading solar module manufacturers, ranked among the Top 4 solar module manufacturers in India by JMK Research & Analytics and among the Global Top 30 by Wood Mackenzie. Established with a vision to accelerate India's renewable energy transition, Gautam Solar combines indigenous innovation, advanced manufacturing, and strong R&D to deliver world-class solar modules.

MISSION
To provide high-quality, affordable solar energy solutions that empower Indian farmers, homeowners, businesses, and utilities to achieve energy independence and sustainability.

MANUFACTURING
Gautam Solar operates two fully automated, state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities:

1. Haridwar Factory — Plot-67 & 68, Sec-8A, SIDCUL, Haridwar, Uttarakhand (1.5 GW capacity)
2. Bhiwani Factory — 7 KM Milestone, Tosham Road, Bhiwani, Haryana (1.7 GW capacity)
Total operational capacity: 3.2 GW (as of Q1 2025)

Upcoming expansion: 5 GW solar cell manufacturing plant in Malanpur, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh (Rs 4000 crore investment)

PRODUCTS
- G12 R TOPCon Solar Modules (620–640 Wp, up to 23.69% efficiency)
- N-Type TOPCon Solar Modules (590–600 Wp, up to 22.84% efficiency)
- Mono PERC Solar Modules (540–550 Wp bifacial and monofacial)
- Solar Power Plant EPC Services
