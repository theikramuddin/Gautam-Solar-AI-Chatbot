BLOG: Complete Guide to Rooftop Solar in India 2024
Published by: Gautam Solar | Category: How-To

With electricity tariffs rising and solar costs falling, rooftop solar has become the single best investment for Indian homeowners and businesses. Here's everything you need to know.

STEP 1: CHECK YOUR ELIGIBILITY
- Own or occupy the building where installation will happen
- Have a sanctioned electricity connection
- Roof must have structural integrity (consult a civil engineer for old buildings)
- Minimum usable roof area: ~10 sq.mt per kWp of solar

STEP 2: ASSESS YOUR ELECTRICITY CONSUMPTION
Look at your electricity bill — specifically the number of units (kWh) consumed per month. A typical Indian household consuming 300 units/month needs approximately a 3 kWp system. A commercial establishment consuming 2000 units/month needs approximately a 20 kWp system.

Rule of thumb: System size (kWp) = Monthly units / 120

STEP 3: UNDERSTAND AVAILABLE SUBSIDIES
PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (2024) — Residential only:
- 1 kWp: Rs 30,000 subsidy
- 2 kWp: Rs 60,000 subsidy
- 3 kWp: Rs 78,000 subsidy (maximum for residential)
- Above 3 kWp: No additional central subsidy, but state subsidies may apply

The subsidy is available only for grid-connected systems installed by empanelled vendors with ALMM-approved modules like Gautam Solar.

STEP 4: SELECT YOUR SYSTEM
Net Metering System: Excess solar power fed back to grid, you receive credit on your electricity bill. Most common for residential and commercial.
Off-Grid System: Includes batteries, works without grid. More expensive, suited for unreliable grid areas.
Hybrid System: Grid-connected with battery backup. Best of both worlds.

STEP 5: CHOOSE YOUR INSTALLER
Verify the installer is MNRE empanelled. Ask for references from past installations. Gautam Solar has a network of 500+ verified installers across India. Call 1800-532-0800 to get connected to an installer in your city.

STEP 6: DOCUMENTATION FOR NET METERING
- Application to your DISCOM/utility
- Electrical drawing of the proposed system
- NOC from the building owner (if tenant)
- Proof of identity and address
- Passport photos

Processing time: 15–45 days depending on your DISCOM.

WHAT TO EXPECT AFTER INSTALLATION:
- Your electricity bill drops by 70–100% depending on solar generation and consumption patterns
- Excess units are credited to your account (net metering)
- Annual generation report available via inverter monitoring app
- Minimal maintenance — mainly periodic cleaning
